How it plays

Each hexagon is a ball spot with six segments where players stand. Your side spends a pool of actions each turn: moving costs one per hexagon, plus one for each opponent in it and one more with the ball; changing segment within your hexagon, passes, shots, tackles and reserves cost one each.

Select a pistachio to see where it can go (the numbers are costs). With the ball, step into another hexagon to dribble, or use a ring to pass or shoot. Opponents in a hexagon you dribble into duel you; opponents in a passing lane may cut it out. Dice and player stats decide.

To tackle, stand in the ball’s hexagon and tap the ball. A player who loses a tackle can’t tackle again that turn.

Reserves are actions you leave on a player for the opponent’s turn: they press dribblers next to them, stretch for nearby passes and grab loose balls. Losing the ball ends your turn.
